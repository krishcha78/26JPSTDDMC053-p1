About Our Institution

More than two decades of technological leadership, rigorous research, and transformative engineering education.

Our Founding & Heritage

Founded in 1998 under the visionary leadership of eminent educationists and technocrats, ABC College of Engineering was instituted to address the growing national requirement for highly trained computer scientists and engineering specialists.

Over the last 28 years, the college has expanded across a 40-acre lush green campus in Sitapura, Jaipur, establishing state-of-the-art research centers in Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Systems, and Robotics. With an autonomous curriculum aligned with Rajasthan Technical University and AICTE norms, our graduates serve in premier global tech organizations and top graduate schools.

Our student body represents over 18 Indian states, fostering a rich, vibrant, and multi-cultural academic environment dedicated to knowledge creation and ethical engineering leadership.

Institutional Mission

  • Deliver world-class undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs anchored in foundational sciences and modern computational technologies.

World-Class Campus Facilities

Every resource required for intense academic focus, athletic vigor, and comfortable campus living.

Central Digital Library

Houses over 55,000 print volumes, IEEE/ACM digital repository access, quiet study carrels, and 24/7 air-conditioned reading halls.

High-Tech Computing Centers

Equipped with 600+ high-end workstations, NVIDIA GPU compute clusters for deep learning, dual gigabit fiber connections, and licensed software suites.

Tagore Convocation Auditorium

An acoustically treated 1,500-seat theater with stage lighting and 4K digital projection, hosting national conferences and cultural festivals.

Olympic Athletic Arena

Features synthetic basketball and tennis courts, standard cricket/football grounds, an indoor badminton arena, and a modern gymnasium.

Student Residential Hostels

Separate, secure residential towers for boys and girls with furnished rooms, high-speed Wi-Fi, hygienic dining facilities, and 24/7 medical supervision.
