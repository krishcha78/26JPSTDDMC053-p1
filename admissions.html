Admissions & Enrollment

Join our vibrant academic community. Follow the streamlined four-step enrollment procedure for the 2026-27 academic session.

Four-Step Admission Process

Transparent, merit-centric, and fully digital application pathway.

STEP 1

Online Registration

Complete the online application form below with your academic details, contact records, and chosen degree specialization.

STEP 2

Document Verification

Upload or submit your 10th and 12th grade marksheets, entrance test scores (JEE Main / REAP / GATE), and identity credentials.

STEP 3

Counseling & Offer

Merit lists are declared. Qualified applicants receive an official admission offer and counseling schedule for branch selection.

STEP 4

Enrollment & Induction

Confirm your seat with initial fee payment, receive your student identity number, and attend the week-long campus induction program.
